## Before You Enter

Workroom is an interactive experience, not a traditional website. To get the intended experience:

- Use a **laptop or desktop** and keep the browser maximized/full-screen.
- **Interact with the environment** — clicking and exploring is part of the experience.
- Avoid switching tabs or minimizing the browser, as the experience may pause.
- To access **Chapter 2**, select any position **except Student**.
- Workroom is still under development, so you may encounter minor inconsistencies or contradictions.
